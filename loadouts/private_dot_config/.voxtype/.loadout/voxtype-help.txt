Voxtype is installed. Hold fn (Globe), speak, release: the text is typed where
your cursor is.

First-time setup
  1. voxtype configure
     Picks the speech model, confirms the hotkey (fn), and walks through the
     permissions.
  2. sudo voxtype setup app-bundle
     Creates /Applications/Voxtype.app. Needs sudo.
  3. Permissions: System Settings > Privacy & Security, turn on Voxtype under
     - Accessibility   (types the text)
     - Input Monitoring (the fn hotkey)
     - Microphone      (macOS asks the first time you record)
     These belong to Voxtype.app. Don't test by running `voxtype` in a
     terminal: that gives the permissions to the terminal app instead.

Start it
  open /Applications/Voxtype.app
  voxtype status            # "idle" means ready

Troubleshooting
  Logs:        tail -f ~/Library/Logs/voxtype/stdout.log
  Mic prompt:  tccutil reset Microphone io.voxtype.daemon
               then open the app and record again
  Stop:        pkill -x voxtype-bin
  "voxtype-osd not found" is harmless: it only means no on-screen indicator.
  The "input group" warning from `voxtype setup check` is Linux only.
